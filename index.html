✅ Project Requirements

For your assigned project, you must deliver the following:

1. Simulator Experiments & Screenshots 20 pts
Requirement: Conduct 5-10 different experiments with varying inputs and configurations
Deliverable: Screenshot evidence of each experiment showing different results
Scope: Test edge cases, normal ranges, and boundary conditions
Documentation: Brief caption for each screenshot explaining what's being tested
2. Performance Data Collection 10 pts
Requirement: Record all metrics displayed by the simulator
Deliverable: Excel spreadsheet or table with measurements
Metrics to Collect: Cycles, hit rates, access times, bandwidth, delays, efficiency percentages, etc.
Format: Organized table with clear column headers and row labels
3. Analysis Report 40 pts
Requirement: Write comprehensive analysis of findings
Length: 3-5 pages (excluding screenshots)
Format: Word/PDF, 12pt font, 1-inch margins, 1.5 spacing
Components:
• Executive summary (1 paragraph)
• Methodology (how you conducted experiments)
• Findings (what you discovered)
• Analysis (why these results matter)
• Graphs/visualizations with captions
4. Trade-Off Analysis 20 pts
Requirement: Identify and explain performance trade-offs
Deliverable: Document or section in report comparing choices
Analysis: Speed vs accuracy, size vs performance, latency vs throughput, etc.
Support: Use your experimental data to justify conclusions
5. Recommendations & Conclusions 10 pts
Requirement: Provide optimization suggestions based on results
Deliverable: 1-2 page recommendations document
Content: Optimal configurations, suggested improvements, real-world applications
Justification: All recommendations must be supported by experimental data

✨ Tips for Success

  • Start Early: Don't wait until day 9 to begin - spread work evenly
  • Experiment Systematically: Test one variable at a time, keep others constant
  • Document Everything: Screenshot every significant result immediately
  • Look for Patterns: Analyze trends across experiments, not just individual numbers
  • Compare Configurations: Test at least 3-4 different setups and compare results
  • Explain Your Thinking: Document why you're testing what you're testing
  • Use Data to Support Claims: Every statement in your report must reference data
  • Professional Presentation: Treat reports as if submitting to a manager
  • Proofread Carefully: Check for grammar, spelling, and formatting errors

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • ❌ Too Few Experiments: Only testing 1-2 configurations (need 5-10 minimum)
  • ❌ Missing Screenshots: Submitting without visual evidence of experiments
  • ❌ Incomplete Data: Missing key metrics or statistics from simulator
  • ❌ Surface-Level Analysis: Just stating what happened without explaining why
  • ❌ No Trade-Off Discussion: Failing to compare speed vs accuracy, size vs performance
  • ❌ Poor Organization: Messy file structure or disorganized report
  • ❌ Unsupported Conclusions: Making claims without data backing
  • ❌ Late Submission: Missing deadline without prior communication
  • ❌ Plagiarism: Copying text or ideas without citation
